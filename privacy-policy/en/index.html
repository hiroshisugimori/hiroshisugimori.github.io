Prompt Editor - Privacy Policy

Developer: hamsoft / Last updated: 2026/10/16

1. About the app and this policy

hamsoft (the developer) explains how data is handled in the Android app プロンプトエディター (Prompt Editor), package jp.hamsoft.prompteditor. This policy applies to this app.

The app helps you create, edit, save, and share prompts for AI. Features include text editing, reusable phrases, placeholder input, image and video attachments, automatic draft saving, text/HTML/ZIP/PDF/PNG output, and sharing to other apps. Some output formats are unavailable with video attachments.

2. Your content, storage, and purposes

Text, placeholder input, drafts, and reusable phrases are stored in the app’s private storage on your device for editing, saving, and reuse. Drafts and phrases are stored in a database; preferences are stored in app settings files.

Selected images and videos are copied into private app storage for attachments, viewing, editing, and output. Drawing and crop files are also stored locally. Deleting an attachment in this app does not delete the original media. Output files are temporarily created in the app’s cache.

The app does not automatically send this editing content to a developer-operated server or an AI service. However, sharing, Google Drive backup, Android backup, and speech recognition may involve external processing as described below. Advertising and billing services also communicate over the network.

3. Sharing, export, and clipboard

When you share or export, text and media included in the selected format are passed to the app you choose. The receiving app or service may store, transmit, or publish them under its own terms and privacy policy.

Copying places text or references to attachment files on the device clipboard. Clipboard access also depends on the OS and other apps. Check personal or confidential information, media content, and metadata before sharing. This app cannot recall or delete data already passed to a recipient.

4. Optional Google Drive backup

When you start backup or restore in Settings and authorize Google account access, the app uses Google authorization services and the Google Drive API. It requests the app-specific data scope (drive.appdata), rather than access to all ordinary Drive files.

The uploaded JSON includes phrase IDs, titles, bodies, creation/update timestamps, shortcut assignments, backup time, and related backup metadata. This phrase backup does not include drafts or attached images/videos. Authorization information and access tokens are used for backup and restore communication. No separate app account registration is required.

The app has no screen for deleting Drive backups. Manage cloud data and access through Google’s controls, such as Manage apps in Google Drive settings. Deleting a local phrase does not delete an existing Drive backup. See the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Android backup and device transfer

The app allows Android backup. Depending on the OS version, device, Google account, and backup settings, app files, databases, and preferences may be included in OS cloud backups or device transfers. The exact scope varies with the OS and other factors. The acknowledged release-history version is excluded from backup.

Manage and delete backups using your device or backup provider’s settings. Clearing local data or uninstalling does not necessarily delete existing cloud backups or data transferred to another device.

6. Advertising: Google AdMob

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display AdMob banner ads. The SDK is initialized at app startup. Banner visibility depends on the ad-removal purchase status; purchasing ad removal does not disable SDK initialization in the current implementation.

For ad delivery, usage analysis, and fraud prevention, the SDK may collect and transmit IP addresses (which may be used to infer approximate location), device/account identifiers such as advertising IDs, ad/app interaction data, and diagnostic information. Advertising IDs can be managed in Android settings. The app’s own code does not add prompt text or attachment files to ad requests.

See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

7. Purchases: Google Play Billing

The app uses Google Play Billing for the one-time ad-removal purchase and checking purchase status. Google Play handles checkout and payment details. The app uses product information, purchase status, and purchase tokens to check entitlement, acknowledge purchases, and restore access. Purchase acknowledgement uses the token with Google Play; ad-removal status is stored locally.

The app does not obtain credit card numbers or similar payment details for the developer. See the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Speech input and other external services

The microphone button launches a speech recognition service installed on the device. Depending on that service and its settings, audio and related information may be sent to external servers for recognition. The app receives recognized text and inserts it into the editor. The app itself does not record or store audio.

Media selection, camera capture, and sharing use OS or selected-app functionality, subject to those providers’ data practices.

9. Personal information, retention, and deletion

Ordinary editing does not require registration with your name or email address. No app-specific code was found that reads contacts, precise location, phone numbers, or permanent device identifiers. Personal information you include in your content and data handled by external services are subject to the practices described above.

  • Delete drafts or phrases from their lists. Clear empties the current editor’s text and attachments. Attachment deletion removes app copies; original media remains.
  • Clear app storage in Android settings or uninstall to delete private local app data. Clear cache through OS settings.
  • Delete saved output, data shared to other apps, original media, Drive backups, and OS backups separately at their respective destinations.

Saved app content has no fixed automatic deletion deadline. Cache may be removed by the OS or user. Retention by external services follows their own policies.

10. Security

The app uses private app storage and Android access controls, granting recipients file-read access when sharing. Google Drive communication uses HTTPS. The app does not provide its own encryption of stored files or password protection for exports. Use device screen locks and OS updates as well. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Children’s privacy

If a child uses the app, parents or guardians should review the content entered, attached, or shared and the use of external services. Data handling by advertising SDKs and other services is described above. Contact the developer below if you have concerns about a child’s personal information.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when features or services change. Revisions and the last-updated date will be posted on this page.
